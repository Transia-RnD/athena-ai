The function performs a full DFS over all nodes, checking four distinct invariants before comparing a locally-built `expectedSeqSupport` map against the state-var `seqSupport`. It's a complex query (trie walk) returning `bool`. Mapping each check to closed `let`/`guard_return`/`ret` steps:
